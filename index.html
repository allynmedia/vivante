The month's real story: a staged campaign rebuild, not a single pause

Account-wide — Google Ads, Newport

Almost every raw-volume metric fell in August — impressions, clicks, sessions, reach — while almost every outcome metric rose. Daily-level data and the account's change history now explain exactly why.

Google's own campaign AI was serving ads to the wrong audience

Google Ads' automated targeting began pushing Newport's campaigns toward low-income housing search intent — despite Vivante offering no low-income housing of any kind. Fixing it required a full account pause, not a single-campaign edit.

The actual outage: two clean days, not a week

Impressions dropped to 292 on August 10th (from 1,454 the day before), then to zero on August 11th and 12th — cost dropped to zero the same two days. Serving resumed August 13th at 3,910 impressions, back above pre-pause levels almost immediately.

What spans the full month is a rolling series of deliberate pauses

Individual campaigns were paused throughout August, not just during the two-day outage: nonbrand search and the "08/2026 - Phase out" campaign on Aug 1–3, YouTube and Memory Care terms on Aug 10 and 13, then YouTube conversions (Aug 18), PerfMax (Aug 20), and Orange County Assisted Living terms (Aug 26). This reads as a staged rebuild across the month, which fits the new PerfMax and phase-out campaigns already visible in the account.

The Aug 17 conversion spike now has a plausible mechanical explanation: serving resumed Aug 13, and conversions typically lag clicks by several days as leads move through the funnel and get attributed. A spike roughly four days after resumption is consistent with that lag, not a separate event — though it's still worth confirming against the specific campaigns that came back online.

Account governance: retiring the legacy setup

Newport only

Lead-quality issue traced to the old account structure

Calls from clearly misaligned audiences — people searching for housing assistance rather than luxury senior living — were traced most likely to broad-match keyword bleed and PMax placement behavior inherited from the prior "Elk"-era setup. This is the same pattern behind August's outage: Google's own campaign AI was actively pushing toward low-income housing search intent, something Vivante does not offer in any form. Account-level negative keywords (poverty, homeless, section eight, affordable) have been added on top of the fix to close the gap structurally.

Account access from the prior vendor relationship is still active and in use

The account's change history shows campaign pauses made August 1–3 by users on the elkhq.com domain, ahead of Allyn's own team pausing additional campaigns on August 10 and 13. Whether that access is a legitimate, ongoing arrangement or a leftover permission that should be revoked is worth confirming directly — it's a separate governance question from the campaign-naming cleanup already underway.

This is the direct explanation for the volume-down/conversions-up pattern above. A two-day full stoppage (Aug 11–12) plus a rolling series of individual campaign pauses through the month accounts for most of August's drop in impressions, clicks, and sessions; the negative-keyword fix and the account running clean afterward account for the stronger conversion and call numbers once serving resumed on Aug 13.

Sitewide CTR improved even as impressions fell
